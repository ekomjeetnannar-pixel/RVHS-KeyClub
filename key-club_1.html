ABOUT US

Service, leadership, and character

Key Club is River Valley High School's largest student-run service organization, sponsored by our local Kiwanis club. Members plan and carry out volunteer projects that support our campus, our neighbors, and organizations across the community.

Beyond service hours, Key Club is a place to build friendships, take on leadership roles, and represent River Valley at district and international Key Club events throughout the year.

01

Community service

Regular volunteer projects on and off campus, logged toward service hour requirements.

02

Leadership development

Officer and committee roles open to members at every grade level.

03

Community connections

Partnerships with local nonprofits, Kiwanis, and neighboring Key Clubs.
