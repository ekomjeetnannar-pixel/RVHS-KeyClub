What Key Club actually is

Key Club International is the largest student-led service organization in the world, and it's built on a simple idea: students are capable of real leadership right now, not someday. Every Key Club is sponsored by a local Kiwanis club, which backs our projects and connects us to a network of service clubs across the globe.

At River Valley, that means Falcons planning their own service projects, running their own meetings, and building the kind of resume line that comes from doing the work, not just showing up for a photo.

The four pillars

Everything Key Club does traces back to these. They're not a slogan, they're how we decide what to work on.

Leadership

Officer roles, committee leads, and project heads are held by students. If you want to plan something and see it through, there's a seat for you.

Service

Hands-on projects in the community: cleanups, drives, fundraisers, and partnerships with local organizations that need extra hands.

Character

Showing up consistently, following through on commitments, and treating service as a habit rather than a one-time event.

Inclusiveness

No tryouts, no minimum GPA to join, no experience required. If you're a Falcon who wants to help, you're already qualified.
