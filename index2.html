Section 01

Our role in Division 14

Why our division matters to the schools and communities we serve.

Setting a standard for student leadership

Division 14 gives students opportunities to lead with responsibility, commitment, and follow-through. Leadership is more than holding a title, it means serving others and setting an example across our clubs.

Connecting schools and communities

Our clubs serve different communities, but Division 14 brings them together. Through shared events and service projects, members can make an impact beyond their own campus.

Creating a culture where service is normal

We encourage service to become a consistent part of student life, so members stay involved and engaged well beyond a single event.

Strengthening our Key Clubs

Division 14 supports its individual clubs through communication, resources, training, collaboration, and division-wide opportunities that help each club grow.

Developing the next generation of leaders

Members build teamwork, responsibility, communication, and leadership skills that extend far beyond their time in Key Club.

Section 03

Get involved

How events, service, and volunteer opportunities work across Division 14.

1

Events get coordinated first

Event details are worked out with participating clubs and Division 14 leadership before anything is announced, so plans are solid before members commit.

2

Opportunities are shared through official channels

Volunteer opportunities and event sign-ups are shared through Division 14 clubs and official communication channels, so keep an eye on your club and our socials.

3

Members honor their commitments

Members are expected to follow through on the commitments they make, and to communicate with event leadership in advance if plans change.

Why serve with Division 14?

  • Build real leadership experience across multiple clubs
  • Meet members from schools outside your own campus
  • Add consistent, meaningful service to your year
  • Connect with the wider CNH District network
